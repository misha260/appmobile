## Slide 1.2 — Brand Archetype & Personality Matrix

**Eyebrow:** Brand Foundation / 02
**Headline:** 80% Explorer. 20% Jester. 100% Pyle.

**Intro:**
Pyle's personality blends two archetypes in a fixed ratio. The Explorer leads: it gives every message its purpose. The Jester follows: it gives every message its spark. Get the ratio wrong and we sound either like a spec sheet or like a joke. Get it right and we sound like the friend who talks you into the thing you'll love.

**Column A — Explorer (80%) · colour cue: Pyle Red**
- *Definition:* Explorer brands help you discover the world. Their products free your individuality, initiative and excitement, and turn experiences into growth.
- **Desire:** Fulfillment · **Reputation:** Curious · **Gift:** Discovery
- **Emotional territory:** Love · Passion · Fulfillment · Curious · Discovery
- **Personality traits:** Encouraging · Confident · Relatable · Experienced

**Column B — Jester (20%) · colour cue: Accent Yellow**
- *Definition:* Jester brands invite you to live in the moment, just for the fun of it. They worry less about long-term outcomes and entertain along the way.
- **Desire:** Joy · **Reputation:** Entertaining · **Gift:** Perspective
- **Emotional territory:** Joy · Energy · Entertaining · Happiness · Hope
- **Personality traits:** Optimistic · Laidback · Nudging · Irreverent

**Actionable tone rules**

| Trait | We sound like… | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| Encouraging (Explorer) | A friend who says "you've got this." | Lead with what the customer can *do*, not what the product *is*. "Start your first podcast tonight." |
| Confident (Explorer) | Someone who's done this for 45 years. | State facts plainly. No hedging, no hype. "100 W peak. Bluetooth 5.0. Done." |
| Relatable (Explorer) | A fellow hobbyist, not a salesperson. | Use everyday words. Explain jargon the first time it appears. |
| Experienced (Explorer) | A trusted guide. | Give one useful tip per touchpoint. Max Volume delivers it. |
| Optimistic (Jester) | Someone who sees the fun first. | Frame upgrades and first purchases as beginnings, not risks. |
| Laidback (Jester) | Relaxed, never pushy. | No false urgency, countdown pressure or fear-based selling. |
| Nudging (Jester) | A playful push off the fence. | Short, active invitations: "Plug in. Turn up. Get into it!" |
| Irreverent (Jester) | A wink, not a sneer. | Light wordplay and sound puns, at most one per piece. Never at the customer's expense. |

**The 80/20 rule in copy:**
In any piece of copy, roughly four sentences inform, guide or inspire (Explorer) for every one that delights (Jester). Headlines may lean Jester. Specs, safety, legal and support copy are always 100% Explorer, with zero jokes.

## Slide 1.4 — Tone of Voice & Copywriting Guidelines

**Eyebrow:** Brand Foundation / 04
**Headline:** Talk like a friend who knows gear.

**Our voice in four words:** Encouraging. Clear. Playful. Honest.

**Core copy principles**
1. **Lead with the passion, then prove it.** Open with what the customer gets to do, then back it up with the spec.
2. **Short beats long.** Headlines: 2–6 words. Bullets: one benefit and one proof each. Sentences: under 20 words.
3. **Active, second person.** "You", "your", "let's". Avoid "users" and "consumers".
4. **Numbers are proof.** Use real, verified figures: watts (state RMS or peak), frequency response, battery hours, IP rating.
5. **One wink per piece.** One pun or playful line per asset, at most. Never in safety, legal or support copy.
6. **Truth is non-negotiable.** Every claim must be substantiated, region-correct and approved (see Section 3).

**Product listing formula**
- **Title:** `Pyle [Product Name] — [Key Spec], [Key Feature], [Use Case]`
  *Example:* "Pyle 3.5" 2-Way Indoor/Outdoor Speaker System — IP67 Waterproof, 70–21 kHz, Universal Wall Mount"
- **Hook line (1 sentence):** the passion it unlocks.
- **3–5 bullets:** `BENEFIT IN CAPS: proof in plain language.`
- **What's in the box** · **Tech specs table** · **Compatibility** · **Warranty & guarantee statement (region-specific; see Section 3)**

**Do's and Don'ts: product listings**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Weatherproof to IP67, so the party keeps going when the sky doesn't cooperate." | "Totally waterproof! Use it anywhere!" (overclaims; IP67 has defined limits) |
| "100 W peak / 50 W RMS power" | "Massive 1000 W!!!" (unqualified, inflated or unverifiable wattage) |
| "Pairs with Bluetooth 5.0 phones, tablets and laptops." | "Works with everything." |
| "Setup takes about 5 minutes. A step-by-step guide is in the box." | "Easy setup!" with no proof |
| "Designed in the USA" (badge, used exactly as supplied) | "Made in the USA", "American-made", or editing the badge wording |
| Spell out units at first use: "Hz (hertz)" in beginner-facing copy | Stacking unexplained acronyms in a single bullet |
| Region-correct spelling, units, currency and guarantee wording | Copy-pasting US listings into CA, EU, UK or AU storefronts |

**Do's and Don'ts: marketing & advertising**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Get Into It!" as the primary sign-off on campaign assets | Rewriting or re-punctuating the tagline ("Get into it", "GET INTO IT!!") |
| "Bigger bang. Smaller buck." for value-led messaging, backed by real pricing | "Cheapest on the market" or "best price guaranteed" without substantiation |
| Show genuine reference prices for promotions (per regional rules) | Fake "was" prices, invented discounts or perpetual "sales" |
| Invite: "Plug in. Turn up. Get into it!" | Pressure: "Only 2 left! Buy NOW or miss out forever!" (unless true and verifiable) |
| Use real customer reviews, gathered and displayed lawfully | Incentivised, fabricated, cherry-picked or undisclosed-incentive reviews |
| Max Volume for tips, onboarding and helpful nudges | Max Volume in price claims, legal copy, safety warnings or complaints handling |
| Clearly label paid partnerships (#ad / "Paid partnership") | Hiding influencer payments or gifted-product relationships |
| Inclusive casting and language | Jokes about skill level, age, gender, body or accent |

**Headline bank (approved)**
- Get Into It!
- The next thing you'll love.
- Bigger bang. Smaller buck.
- Buy so you can try.
- Radiating sound into every space.
- Don't just listen — feel the sound!

## Slide 2.1 — Logo System & Clearspace Rules

**Eyebrow:** Visual Identity / 01
**Headline:** One wordmark. Three ways to wear it.

**Intro:**
The refreshed PYLE wordmark keeps the heritage letterforms and sharpens them for screens, packaging and product. It is built on a precise construction grid and judged against five principles: Simplicity, Memorability, Timelessness, Versatility and Relevance.

**1. Primary Wordmark: PYLE (horizontal)**
- Use it as the default on every touchpoint: packaging fronts, web headers, advertising, social avatars and documents.
- **Approved colourways:**
  - Pyle Red on Cream (hero)
  - Pyle Red on Deep Maroon (hero, dark)
  - Black on White / Light Grey (mono, light)
  - White on Black (mono, dark)
  - Cream or Accent Yellow on photography (only where contrast passes; see Slide 2.2)
- **Registered symbol:** Use ® only in jurisdictions where the PYLE mark is registered. Where registration is pending or not held, use ™ or no symbol. Falsely claiming registration is an offence in several of our markets (including the UK and Australia). Brand Legal holds the master registration list.

**2. Stacked Icon: PY / LE**
- A two-line monogram for small square formats: app icons, favicons, social avatars, product badges, embossing and hang-tags.
- Never use it as a replacement for the primary wordmark on packaging fronts or in advertising headlines.

**3. Vertical Lockup: P-L-Y-E**
- A single-column vertical lockup for tall, narrow surfaces: speaker cabinet grille badges, tower speakers, mic stands, side gussets and tape.
- Reads top to bottom. Never rotate the horizontal wordmark 90° to imitate it.

**Clearspace (48HD standard)**
- **X = the cap height of the "P" in the wordmark as used.**
- Primary wordmark: minimum clearspace of **1X** on all sides.
- Stacked icon: minimum clearspace of **0.5X** on all sides.
- Vertical lockup: minimum clearspace of **0.5X** left and right, **1X** top and bottom.
- No text, graphics, edges, folds or Pulse of Play rings may enter the clearspace. Rings may run *behind* the logo only on full-bleed brand backgrounds, and only where the logo passes contrast.

**Minimum size (48HD standard)**

| Asset | Print | Digital |
|---|---|---|
| Primary wordmark | 20 mm wide | 80 px wide |
| Stacked icon | 10 mm wide | 32 px wide (16 px favicon uses a simplified, pixel-hinted file) |
| Vertical lockup | 8 mm wide | 24 px wide |
| "Designed in the USA" badge | 12 mm diameter | 48 px diameter |

**"Designed in the USA" Badge**
- A circular roundel with the US flag at its centre and "DESIGNED IN THE USA" set around the ring.
- **Approved versions:** Dark outline on light/kraft backgrounds; white on Pyle Red.
- **Placement:** On packaging, the Trust panel and the lower-right of the Product panel; on product pages, next to warranty/guarantee information; in advertising, as an endorsement, never as the headline.
- **Rules:**
  - Never change the wording to "Made in", "Built in" or "Engineered in".
  - Never use the badge on a product that was not genuinely designed by Pyle's US team.
  - Always show the true country of manufacture elsewhere on the pack (e.g., "Made in China").
  - Never place the badge directly beside the country-of-origin line where the two could be read as one claim.
  - **Canada:** packaging sold in Canada carries the French badge, **"CONÇU AUX ÉTATS-UNIS"**, either as a bilingual roundel or paired with the English version at equal or greater prominence (see Slide 3.1).
  - **Other languages:** EU language variants of the badge are optional. Translated badges are produced only from the master file by 48HourDigital.

**Logo misuse: never**
- Stretch, condense, skew, outline or add effects (shadow, glow, bevel, gradient).
- Recolour outside the approved colourways, or set the logo in Parafina or any typeface.
- Use the deprecated "Visual Brand Now" logo, or the pre-refresh "PYLE® Experience Life!" lock-up.
- Place it on busy photography without a contrast-safe zone.
- Combine it with a sub-brand, retailer logo or tagline inside its clearspace.

## Slide 2.3 — Typography Hierarchy

**Eyebrow:** Visual Identity / 03
**Headline:** Three voices, one conversation.

**The system**

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display & headings | **Parafina** | Medium (S) / Regular (S) | Headlines, taglines, product names, section titles |
| Handwritten accent | **Delicious Handrawn** | Regular | One or two words of emphasis, Max Volume speech bubbles, the "Into" in "Get Into It!" |
| Body copy | **Manrope** | Medium (Regular, SemiBold and Bold for UI states) | Body text, specs, UI, legal, packaging technical panels |

**Hierarchy (digital, 48HD standard)**
- **H1 / Hero:** Parafina Medium, 56–96 px, line-height 1.0, tracking −1%
- **H2:** Parafina Medium, 36–48 px, line-height 1.1
- **H3:** Parafina Regular, 24–28 px, line-height 1.2
- **Accent:** Delicious Handrawn, 110–120% of the adjacent heading size, maximum three words
- **Body:** Manrope Medium, 16–18 px, line-height 1.5
- **Caption / legal:** Manrope Medium, 12–14 px, line-height 1.4 (never below 12 px on screen)
- **Buttons / labels:** Manrope SemiBold, 14–16 px, sentence case

**Hierarchy (print, 48HD standard)**
- Packaging product name: Parafina Medium, min. 14 pt
- Packaging body/technical: Manrope Medium, min. 6 pt. Regulated statements follow the local legal minimum size where one exists.
- Brochure body: Manrope Medium, 9–11 pt

**Pairing rules**
- Delicious Handrawn is an *accent*, never a sentence. Use it on one word or phrase per composition.
- Never set Parafina below 14 px / 9 pt, or in all caps for more than three words.
- Taglines are always set in sentence or title case exactly as approved: "Get Into It!" · "The next thing you'll love." · "Bigger bang. Smaller buck."

**Fallbacks**

| Typeface | Web fallback stack | Print / Office fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Parafina | `"Parafina", "Manrope", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` | Manrope SemiBold (licensed, no cost) |
| Delicious Handrawn | `"Delicious Handrawn", "Comic Neue", cursive` | No print substitute. Outline the accent as artwork from the master file. |
| Manrope | `"Manrope", "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` | Arial (Office documents only) |

**Licensing & language support**
- Manrope and Delicious Handrawn are open-licence (Google Fonts) families. Parafina is a commercial typeface. Confirm desktop, web, app and packaging seats before regional rollout.
- **Glyph check before localisation:** Every font must render the full character set for each market language. French: é è ê ë à â ç î ï ô û ù ü ÿ œ æ « ». German: ä ö ü ß, and capital ẞ where used. If Delicious Handrawn is missing a required glyph, the accent word is re-chosen or hand-lettered from the master. Never mix fonts inside a single word.
- **French typography:** Canadian French follows Office québécois de la langue française (OQLF) spacing (space before ":" only). France uses a narrow no-break space before ! ? ; : and around « guillemets ».

## Slide 2.4 — "Pulse of Play" Graphic System & Max Volume Mascot

**Eyebrow:** Visual Identity / 04
**Headline:** The heartbeat of Pyle.

**The Pulse of Play**
"Pulse of Play" is our visual design system. It captures the dynamic essence of Pyle's audio experience in concentric soundwave rings that radiate outward like sound from a speaker cone or the grooves of a record. It stands for innovation, quality and the joy of sound that echoes through every product we create. With Pulse of Play, every beat and note is not just heard, but felt, across every visual touchpoint.

**Line:** *Radiating sound into every space.*

**Construction rules**
- Rings are **concentric and evenly spaced**. Stroke weight and gap are equal (1 : 1) by default. A 1 : 1.5 gap is permitted on large formats.
- The ring centre (the "source") sits on a meaningful point: the product's driver, a performer's mouth or microphone, the logo, or deliberately off-canvas so arcs sweep in from a corner.
- **Approved colourways:**
  - Pyle Red rings on Maroon (hero)
  - Accent Yellow rings on Cream (light/playful)
  - Black rings on kraft (master carton, one-colour)
  - Deep Maroon rings on White
- **Interaction with photography:** Rings may wrap around or emerge from behind a cut-out subject to create depth (as in the lifestyle and consumer-pack art). They must never cross a face or obscure the product.
- **Motion:** In video and digital, rings pulse outward from the source at tempo. Use 1–2 seconds per cycle, ease-out, and loop seamlessly. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: provide a static frame.
- **Never:** distort rings into ellipses (except for perspective on 3D packshots), add gradients, mix more than two ring colours, or use rings as a text background below 7 : 1 contrast.

**Max Volume: our Megaphone Mascot**
Max is a character who embodies the spirit of Pyle's audio innovation and community engagement. Max is the voice of the brand. He delivers helpful tooltips, inspiring quotes and engaging content directly to our audience.

**Where Max appears**
- Tooltips and "Tip:" call-outs in user guides, quick-start cards and product pages
- Onboarding moments, empty states and success messages in digital products
- Social content, packaging inserts, and community or event content

**Max's voice**
- Short, upbeat, useful. Always one actionable idea. Written in Delicious Handrawn inside a Cream speech bubble.
- *Approved examples:*
  - "Don't just listen — feel the sound!"
  - "Try this feature for a sound that's as big as life!"
  - "Tip: Boost your bass by adjusting the EQ settings!"

**Max Volume rules**
1. Max appears **once per layout**, maximum.
2. Max always **speaks toward the content** he refers to. The megaphone points at the bubble or the feature.
3. Maintain **0.5× Max's height** in clearspace around the character and bubble.
4. Minimum size: **24 mm / 64 px** tall.
5. Never recolour Max, change his expression set, add accessories, crop his face, or animate him off-model. All poses come from the master character sheet.
6. **Max never delivers:** prices or promotions, safety warnings, legal or compliance statements, warranty or guarantee terms, or apologies and complaint responses. Those are always plain Manrope copy.
7. Max is not used next to the "Designed in the USA" badge or regulatory marks.

## Slide 2.6 — Packaging Standard: the 5-Pillar Panel System

**Eyebrow:** Visual Identity / 06
**Headline:** Every box tells the whole story in five panels.

**Intro:**
Every Pyle pack, from kraft master carton to full-colour retail box, is organised into five content pillars. Customers find the same information in the same place on every product, in every market.

**The five pillars**

- The wordmark appears on at least four faces.

## Slide 3.2 — 🇪🇺 European Union (EU)

**Eyebrow:** Regional Requirements / European Union
**Headline:** The price you see is the price you pay. Always.

**1. Pricing: VAT-inclusive, always**
- **Rule:** Every consumer-facing price must show the **final price including VAT** and all unavoidable charges. This applies to product pages, category pages, ads, social posts, emails, comparison feeds and the cart. VAT is never added at checkout. (Price Indication Directive 98/6/EC; Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU.)
- **Delivery costs** are shown before the order button. If they can't be calculated in advance, say that they will be added.
- **Price reductions (Omnibus Directive 2019/2161):** Any announced discount must show the **lowest price applied in the previous 30 days** as the reference price. *Approved format:* "€49.99 — Lowest price in the last 30 days: €59.99".
- **Currency & format by locale:** Germany/France/Spain/Italy **49,99 €** · Netherlands **€ 49,99** · Ireland **€49.99**.
- **Checkout button:** must read **"Order with obligation to pay"** or an unambiguous equivalent ("Buy now" / « Commander avec obligation de paiement » / „Zahlungspflichtig bestellen").

**2. Consumer rights: 14-day right of withdrawal**
- Consumers may withdraw from an online purchase **within 14 days of receiving the goods**, without giving a reason.
  - Pyle must refund all payments, including standard outbound delivery, within 14 days of being notified. The refund may be withheld until the goods are returned or proof of return is supplied.
  - Disclose who pays return shipping. If Pyle doesn't disclose it, Pyle pays.
  - Provide the **EU Model Withdrawal Form**.
  - If the withdrawal information is missing, the period extends by up to **12 months**.
- **Withdrawal function (since 19 June 2026):** Online storefronts must provide a clearly labelled, easily accessible **"Withdraw from contract here"** function that runs throughout the withdrawal period, followed by an on-screen confirmation and an acknowledgement on a durable medium (e.g., email).
- **Approved disclosure copy (EN master; translate for each locale):**
  > **Your right of withdrawal.** You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The withdrawal period expires 14 days after the day on which you, or a third party other than the carrier indicated by you, take physical possession of the goods. To exercise your right of withdrawal, use the "Withdraw from contract here" button in your account or order email, use the model withdrawal form, or send us a clear statement by email to [support address]. We will reimburse all payments received from you, including standard delivery costs, without undue delay and no later than 14 days from the day we are informed of your decision. We may withhold reimbursement until we have received the goods back or you have supplied evidence of having sent them back. [You bear / We bear] the direct cost of returning the goods. You are only liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.
- **Legal guarantee:** A minimum **2-year** legal guarantee of conformity (Directive 2019/771) applies on top of any Pyle commercial warranty. Some member states set longer periods (e.g., Spain and Portugal: 3 years). Under the **Right to Repair Directive (2024/1799)**, applicable from 31 July 2026, the guarantee is extended by 12 months when the consumer chooses repair. Never write copy that implies the Pyle warranty replaces the legal guarantee.

**3. Regulatory marks & product compliance (Trust panel)**
- **CE marking** on product, packaging and documentation, backed by an EU Declaration of Conformity under the applicable laws:
  - **Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU** (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi), including the cybersecurity requirements (EN 18031 series) for internet-connected radio equipment, mandatory since 1 August 2025
  - **EMC Directive 2014/30/EU**
  - **Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU**
  - **RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU** (RoHS compliance is declared through CE. There is no separate consumer-facing RoHS logo requirement. A "RoHS compliant" statement on the technical panel is optional.)
- **WEEE (Directive 2012/19/EU):** The **crossed-out wheeled bin symbol** with a solid bar beneath (EN 50419) goes on the product, or on the packaging if the product is too small. Pyle must be registered with each country's EPR register; Germany's **WEEE-Reg.-Nr. DE** must appear on German product offers.
- **Batteries (Regulation 2023/1542):** Separate-collection symbol on batteries and battery-containing products. Further capacity and removability labelling phases in from 2027.
- **Common charger (Directive 2022/2380):** Portable speakers, headphones, headsets, keyboards and similar rechargeable products sold in the EU must charge via **USB-C**. They must carry the "charger included / not included" pictogram and the charging-power label on pack and online.
- **General Product Safety Regulation (2023/988):** The pack and every online listing must show the manufacturer's name, postal and electronic address, the **EU responsible economic operator** (importer or authorised representative established in the EU), the product identifier (model/batch), and warnings and safety information **in the language(s) of the member state** where it is sold.

- **Placement:** EU marks *replace* US-only marks on EU-variant packs. The FCC logo is optional on US packs and is removed from EU artwork to avoid clutter. Dual-market packs may carry both sets of marks if the Trust panel stays legible.

**4. Language**
- Safety information, instructions and warranty documents must be in the official language(s) of each member state of sale.
- **France (Loi Toubon):** French is mandatory on packaging, instructions, warranties, advertising and the website. English slogans like "Get Into It!" need a French translation of equal legibility. *Candidates:* « Lance-toi ! » · « Ton prochain coup de cœur. » · « Plus de son. Moins de sous. »

- **Impressum (Germany: § 5 DDG; Austria: § 5 ECG):** A statutory provider-identification page, reachable within two clicks from every page and labelled "Impressum". It must list:
  - Full company name and legal form
  - Authorised representative(s)
  - Postal address (no P.O. box)
  - Email address **and** a second fast contact method (phone)
  - Commercial register and number
  - VAT identification number
  - Person responsible for content where applicable

    > **Impressum** · Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG · [Legal entity name, legal form] · [Street, number, postcode, city, country] · Vertreten durch: [Managing director(s)] · Kontakt: Telefon [number], E-Mail [address] · Registereintrag: [Register court], [Register number] · Umsatzsteuer-ID gemäß § 27a UStG: [VAT ID] · WEEE-Reg.-Nr.: DE [number]

  - Non-essential cookies, pixels and SDKs load **only after opt-in**.

  - Germany: § 25 TDDDG.

- **GDPR:**
  - Lawful basis for each processing purpose

  - Data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) handled within one month
  - Records of processing; breach notification within 72 hours
  - EU representative (Art. 27) if Pyle has no EU establishment
  - International transfers to the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses
- **Online dispute resolution:** The EU ODR platform closed on 20 July 2025. Remove any legacy ODR link and text.
- **Accessibility:** Under the **European Accessibility Act**, applicable from 28 June 2025, e-commerce services must be accessible. Meet **EN 301 549** (which incorporates WCAG 2.1 AA). Pyle's standard is WCAG 2.2 AA. Publish an accessibility statement.

## Slide 3.4 — 🇦🇺 Australia (AU)

**Eyebrow:** Regional Requirements / Australia
**Headline:** All-in prices. Guarantees you can't sign away.

**1. Pricing: GST-inclusive by law**
- **Single price rule (Australian Consumer Law s 48):** Every advertised price must be the **total minimum price including 10% GST** and any other unavoidable charges. This applies to product pages, ads, social, email and the cart. Delivery charges are disclosed separately and clearly.
- **Currency:** Australian dollar, displayed as **$49.99** on the .com.au storefront and **A$49.99** on international or mixed-currency surfaces.
- **Was/now pricing:** Reference prices must be genuine prices at which the product was offered for a reasonable period before the sale (ACCC guidance).

**2. Australian Consumer Law (ACL): statutory guarantees**
- Under the ACL, products must be of acceptable quality, fit for purpose, match their description, and come with spare parts and repair facilities for a reasonable time. These **consumer guarantees apply automatically and cannot be excluded, restricted or modified**.
- **Mandatory warranty wording:** Any "warranty against defects" (including the Pyle limited warranty card, user guide and product-page warranty text) **must include this exact text** (Competition and Consumer Regulations 2010, reg 90):
  > Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

  - What Pyle will do and what the customer must do to claim
  - Pyle's name, business address, phone number and email
  - The claim period
  - Who bears the cost of a claim
  - That the benefits are **in addition to** other rights and remedies under the law
- **Prohibited language (never use in AU, and best avoided everywhere):**
  - "No refunds" / "No refunds on sale items"
  - "Warranty void if…" (where it would exclude statutory rights)
  - "Manufacturer's warranty only: we are not responsible"
  - "Returns accepted only with original packaging"
  - "Warranty is limited to 12 months" (stated as a limit on rights)
  - "All sales final"
  - "Store credit only for faulty items"
- **Change of mind:** There is no cooling-off right for online purchases. A change-of-mind return policy is optional. If Pyle offers one, it must be honoured as advertised and must not imply that statutory rights depend on it.
- **Unfair contract terms:** These are prohibited and carry penalties since 9 November 2023. Our terms and conditions are reviewed for AU.

- **Spelling:** Australian English, following British conventions: colour, organise, centre, catalogue, aluminium, licence, tyre, programme.
- **Vocabulary:** "postcode", "mobile", "trolley/cart" (either is acceptable; use "cart" online), "ute" in Automotive, "esky" in Outdoor where appropriate.
- **Formats:** Date **DD/MM/YYYY**; metric units only on primary specs, with imperial screen and driver sizes permitted in brackets; AUD currency.
- **Taglines:** "Get Into It!", "The next thing you'll love." and "Bigger bang. Smaller buck." are all approved for AU unchanged.

**4. Regulatory marks & product safety (Trust panel)**
- **RCM (Regulatory Compliance Mark):** Required on electrical, EMC and radio-communications products. The **responsible supplier must be an Australian entity** registered on the national EESS database. In-scope (Level 3) electrical equipment, such as some power supplies and chargers, must be registered with a certificate of conformity before sale.
- **Button/coin batteries (mandatory standards):** Remote controls and any product using button/coin batteries require a secure battery compartment, compliance testing, and **warnings on the product, packaging and instructions**, plus online listings. Highly relevant to remote-equipped audio and video products.
- Plugs: AS/NZS 3112 Type I on all mains products.

**5. Marketing & privacy**
- **Spam Act 2003:**
  - Commercial email/SMS needs **consent** (express, or narrowly inferred).
  - Every message must accurately **identify** Pyle and include a functional **unsubscribe**, honoured within **5 business days**.
  - No address-harvesting software or harvested lists.
- **Privacy Act 1988 & Australian Privacy Principles (APPs):**
  - APP-compliant privacy policy (collection, use, disclosure, overseas disclosure to the US, access and correction, complaints).
  - Collection notices at point of capture.
  - Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
  - Under the 2024 amendments: a statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy (from June 2025), and transparency on automated decisions in privacy policies (from 10 December 2026).
- **Advertising:** ACL bans misleading or deceptive conduct. Claims ("waterproof", "1000 W", "best value") must be accurate and substantiated. Influencer posts must be clearly disclosed (AANA Code of Ethics; ACCC influencer guidance).
- **Accessibility:** Disability Discrimination Act 1992. Pyle meets **WCAG 2.2 AA**.
